## 1. What the reference does (why it works)

1. **0–11 s hook at the wedding:** the curvy hero (black slip dress) is confronted by the bride, smiles and walks away through staring guests. Before we know anything, we see the "after" body.
2. **Flashback (11–40 s):** a split-screen phone call with the tía and a white flash, then the "flat sister" childhood, sister loses weight, dinner accusation, the failed attempts (shakes, squats, eating plates).
3. **Rock bottom (40–76 s):** doctor tablet ("genética / BBL"), crying in the car, baggy clothes in bed and street, avoiding mirrors and photos.
4. **The secret (76–125 s):** hallway cabinet with the yellow droppers, Brazilian women in Rio, then **golden glowing energy-line VFX** over hips/butt/belly to explain "the signal". Grandmothers grinding maca, then the glow settles on the hips.
5. **Product + transformation (125–156 s):** dropper under the tongue, breakfast and appetite, jeans tight on the thighs, fitted black dress, smiling at the mirror.
6. **Wedding payoff (156–192 s):** every head turns, the bride is upset, then takes her hand, and the hero gives her the bottle.
7. **Social proof + CTA (192–244 s):** bride texting (whip-cut micro-flashes), bride now curvy in the mirror, Brazilian women, unboxing bottles together, a guarantee shot (shield, calendar "3 MONTHS", cash). Bottle in hand with a golden swirl, and a final full-body pose.

Constants: warm amber/brown palette, soft bokeh, characters act (no lip sync), the product is held toward camera with the label readable, and the golden light swirl is the "signal" motif.

### HERO — "Valeria" (Mexican, 27). Needs two sheets of the same face.
- **Face (both sheets):** Mexican mestiza features, warm light-tan *morena clara* skin, oval face, softly rounded cheekbones, dark-brown almond eyes, thick natural dark eyebrows, full lips, small beauty mark under the left eye (identity anchor). Dark-brown hair in a **high voluminous curly bun with loose face-framing curls**. That bun is her silhouette signature in every shot, like the reference.
- **BEFORE sheet:** slender, **straight "ruler" silhouette**: flat hips, flat butt, thin thighs, jeans loose at the waist and seat. No makeup, tired eyes. Wardrobe: fitted black crew tee + mid-blue jeans; alt: grey oversized tee + grey baggy joggers; green tee (phone-call scene).
- **AFTER sheet:** same face and same bun, glam makeup, gold hoops. **Pear-shaped hourglass**: narrow waist, wide hips, full thighs, round lifted glutes, **natural, not surgical**, waist not exaggerated. Wardrobe: black satin slip midi dress with a thigh slit (wedding and finale); black tee + tight jeans (transformation); cream ribbed lounge set (texting scene).
- Sheet layout: face close-up, front full body, 3/4 left, 3/4 right, back view (back view is critical: it carries the before/after).

### SISTER / BRIDE — "Daniela" (Mexican, 30)
Slightly lighter tan skin, long voluminous dark wavy hair with a crystal hair clip, defined brows. Three body states:
1. **Flashback (14–17 s):** curvy pear shape, white tank top + jeans; she was the "curvy sister".
2. **Now / wedding:** **slim, straight figure** after losing weight. Long-sleeve lace mermaid gown and veil; white crop tee + flat jeans in the 21–24 s shots; cream off-shoulder top at dinner.
3. **Ending (192–207 s):** curvy again, glowing; white lace robe, then cream bike shorts in front of the mirror.

### TÍA BRASILEÑA (Brazilian, mid-50s)
Warm brown skin, big natural curly hair, gold hoops, orange-and-cream floral blouse, rust wide-leg trousers, curvy, warm, theatrical. Bright tropical home with plants and fruit.

### Extras
Wedding guests (Mexican family, tuxedos, formal dresses), groom (Mexican, 30s, black tux, beard), female doctor (white coat, tablet), Brazilian women on a Rio street and café (curvy, colourful summer outfits), grandmothers grinding herbs.

### PRODUCT — MUNA VitalDrops (attach `packaging bottle.png` to every product shot)
Amber glass bottle, black rubber dropper with black ribbed collar, bright yellow label: **"muna"** in heavy black lowercase, **"VITALDROPS"** in white with a drop for the O, "All-in-One Multi-Herbal Supplement", five black circles: A Ashwagandha · F Fenugreek · L L-Arginine · MA Maca Root · BR Beet Root.
